# DRAPE — Risk Register

Likelihood and impact are qualitative at Phase 0.1; **severity** combines them and drives investigation priority.

| ID | Category / description | Likelihood | Impact | Severity | Mitigation strategy | Validation required |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| RSK-001 | Technical: monocular webcam reconstruction cannot reliably infer exact 3D body shape or measurements. | High | High | Critical | Do not expose measurements/fit scores; scope output as visual preview; test on representative bodies/devices. | Benchmark error/failure analysis and legal/UX review of claims. |
| RSK-002 | Product: visual preview may be interpreted as fit, size, drape, or comfort assurance. | High | High | Critical | Persistent plain-language limits; separate size guidance; no predictive claims. | Comprehension usability test and content approval. |
| RSK-003 | Technical: garment deformation, pose, collision and occlusion may look implausible. | High | High | Critical | Stage research; define quality gates/fallback imagery; do not promise physical simulation. | Representative visual-quality study across poses/assets. |
| RSK-004 | Operational/licensing: GLB/glTF garment assets may be missing, inconsistent, costly, or unlicensed. | High | High | Critical | Asset specification, provenance inventory, pilot creation pipeline, release gate. | Sample asset audit: geometry, materials, metadata, rights, performance. |
| RSK-005 | Technical: image try-on can distort identity/garment details, fail diverse inputs, or generate unsafe output. | High | High | Critical | Input guidance, evaluation set, moderation/policy, transparent failure, human escalation policy. | Quality/safety/bias evaluation and red-team results. |
| RSK-006 | Financial: model inference, storage and egress costs may exceed value. | Medium | High | High | Rate limits, queues, quotas, telemetry, model comparison and pilot budget cap. | Cost per attempt/session under realistic load and sensitivity model. |
| RSK-007 | Technical: WebGL/camera/model performance and compatibility vary across devices/browsers. | High | Medium | High | Supported matrix, progressive enhancement, feature detection, fallbacks. | Device/browser lab matrix with performance/failure data. |
| RSK-008 | Privacy: camera frames, photos, body-related data and generated images create sensitive-data obligations. | High | High | Critical | Data minimization, granular consent, retention/deletion, access control, DPIA/legal review. | Data map, DPIA, deletion test, penetration/authorization test. |
| RSK-009 | Security: account/order/wardrobe/output data could be exposed or altered. | Medium | High | High | Threat model, least privilege, secure provider review, monitoring/incident plan. | Security assessment and cross-account authorization tests. |
| RSK-010 | Licensing: models, datasets, fonts, editorial photography, and garment assets may restrict commercial use. | Medium | High | High | License/provenance register and approval gate; avoid assumed rights. | Counsel/owner approval for each production asset/model. |
| RSK-011 | Commerce: payment, tax, duties, shipping, returns and consumer rules differ by market. | High | High | Critical | Start with explicit market matrix and supported launch scope. | Provider/legal/ops sign-off per launch market. |
| RSK-012 | Product data: missing or inaccurate sizes, materials, availability, images and attributes undermine commerce/recommendations. | High | High | Critical | Define source-of-truth, data contracts and completeness checks. | Catalog audit and reconciliation test. |
| RSK-013 | AI stylist: sparse/incorrect wardrobe data yields irrelevant or harmful recommendations. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Explain inputs, allow feedback, disclose uncertainty, evaluate quality by cohort. | Offline evaluation and moderated usability test. |
| RSK-014 | Brand: copied reference assets/logos or generic implementation harms IP/positioning. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Use references only for direction; commission/license assets; design review. | Rights review and brand sign-off. |
| RSK-015 | Operational: experimental systems increase support, moderation, incident and deletion-request load. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Define ownership/runbooks, phased access, support taxonomy and kill switches. | Operational readiness exercise and support-volume pilot. |
| RSK-016 | Architecture: premature service/infrastructure complexity conflicts with low-cost modular-monolith constraint. | Medium | Medium | Medium | Keep core modular; isolate workloads only after measurable justification. | Architecture decision record comparing cost/latency/operability. |
